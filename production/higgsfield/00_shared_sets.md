# Shared sets: make these first

Two empty rooms that Sam appears in across every video. Generate each once (Nano Banana Pro, 2K, 16:9), make 4 options, keep the most real-looking one and save it under the name in bold. Every Sam still in the study or boardroom attaches this plate as a reference, so the room, props and light stay identical from week to week.

## set_study.png

```
Empty plate, no people: a warm home study at dusk: dark walnut desk, floor-to-ceiling shelves of worn cloth hardbacks 2.5 m behind the desk, a green-shaded brass banker's lamp on the desk at frame left. Light: one soft key from a window camera-left at 45 degrees, 3200 K tungsten interior, cool 5600 K dusk spill on the far shelves. Lens: 50 mm at f/2, camera at seated eye height (1.2 m), framed so a person can later be placed seated behind the desk, slightly right of centre. Shot on ARRI Alexa 35 with Kodak 2383 print emulation, fine 35 mm grain, slight halation on practical lights. Real wear: scuffs on the desk edge, uneven book spines, dust in the lamp light. No text, no logos, no screens, no microphone. 16:9.
```
