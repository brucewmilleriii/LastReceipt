## G07: US homes with a DVD player

Only years with a published figure are plotted. No in-between years are invented.

| Year | Share | Source |
|---|---|---|

## G10 and G13: subscribers, Netflix vs Blockbuster Online

Netflix (end of quarter, from Netflix earnings releases on SEC EDGAR):
- Q4 2006 6,316,000 and Q3 2007 7,028,000 and Q4 2007 7,479,000: Q4 2007 release https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065280/000119312508010549/dex991.htm
- Q2 2007 6,742,000, a fall of 55,000 in the quarter (so Q1 2007 was about 6,797,000): Q2 2007 release https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065280/000119312507159829/dex991.htm and MediaPost, Jul 24 2007

Blockbuster Online (all online subscribers):
- Dec 31 2006 about 2.2 million: Blockbuster 10-K for 2006 https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1085734/000119312507044360/d10k.htm
- End Q2 2007 3.6 million (up 600,000 in the quarter, so about 3.0 million at end Q1): Forbes, Jul 26 2007 https://www.forbes.com/2007/07/26/blockbuster-total-access-markets-equity-cx_af_0726markets15.html
- End Q3 2007 3.1 million (lost 500,000 over the summer): 9News / AP, late 2007 https://www.9news.com/article/money/business/blockbuster-charts-course-to-digital-age/73-343233573

Not found yet: a sourced Blockbuster figure for end of 2007, so the Blockbuster line stops at Q3 2007.

On screen: "Sources: Netflix and Blockbuster filings; Forbes; AP".

## G03 and G15: store maps

The dots show the spread and the shutdown. They are an illustration, not store addresses: dots are placed by state population. On screen: "Illustration. Dots are not exact store sites." Map outline: US Census Bureau cartographic boundaries via the us-atlas package (public domain data, ISC-licensed package).

G15 year ticker ends at 2019: the Bend franchise became the last Blockbuster in the world in March 2019, when the Perth, Australia store closed (widely reported; confirm a news link before upload).
